# Code of Conduct

Kenning follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This page is the short version and says how to report a problem.

## What is expected

- Be kind and patient, including with people new to crypto or to Rust.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume the other person means well.
- Accept a "no" on a change gracefully; the reasons are usually written down
  in `docs/`.
- Keep the people who use Kenning in mind: they trust it with payments.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, including their addresses,
  balances or transactions, without their permission.
- Asking anyone for a recovery phrase or a private key, for any reason.
- Promoting tokens, airdrops or other projects in issues and pull requests.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and
anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer privately through [@PTK030's GitHub profile](https://github.com/PTK030).
Reports are read in confidence, and the person reporting is protected from
retaliation.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break these
rules, and may warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, anyone who keeps
breaking them, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
